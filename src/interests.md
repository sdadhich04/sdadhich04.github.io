# Interests

These are the technical problems I want to keep working on.

## Bioimpedance and medical sensing
Getting trustworthy electrical measurements from tissue: analog front ends, calibration against reference networks, and the artifacts that hide the real signal.

## TinyML and edge inference
Fitting useful models onto microcontrollers through quantization, pruning and distillation, and keeping inference on the device.

## Neurotech and brain-computer interfaces
Closed-loop systems that decode body or neural signals in real time, and how feedback delay changes what a user can do with them.

## Embedded firmware
Real-time firmware on ESP32-class parts: RTOS task design, sensor drivers, and finding the one wrong flag that breaks an IMU.

## Test automation and sensor reliability
Rigs and scripts that stress hardware the same way every time, and ways to tell a failing sensor from a real change in the signal.

## Wearables
Body-worn sensing that has to work outside the lab, such as IMU and EMG wearables or wearable cardiac monitors.

## Sensing and control for robotics
State estimation and closed-loop control, applied to robots. I haven't built a robot yet.
