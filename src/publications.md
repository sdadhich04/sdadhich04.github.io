## Biomedical sensing instruments

### Thyroid Parathyroid (TPT) - Finder: an AI-enabled, real-time surgical tool to eliminate the leading cause of hypoparathyroidism
Ali Melih Boyun, Beth Yue Ding, **Sparsh Dadhich**
Poster on a research prototype · UW ECE BioRobotics Laboratory · 2026 Hollomon Health Innovation Challenge (Buerk Center)
`PRESENTED`

[Poster (PDF)](/assets/pdf/tpt-finder-poster-2026.pdf)

![Thumbnail of the TPT-Finder poster](/assets/img/tpt-finder-poster-2026.png)

The poster's bioimpedance results, a 0.034% calibration error at 33 kHz (2200 Ω, measured on BioZ v2) and 500+ ex-vivo tissue measurements, come from the instrument work I lead. I ran the ex-vivo study and all of those measurements.

## Sensor reliability & benchmarks

### SHIBench: A Benchmark for Sensor-Level Fault Detection on an Embedded Acquisition Platform
Alperen Cucioglu, **Sparsh Dadhich**, Ameyan Dash, Nicolas Berredo, Gokul Nathan, Joydeep Saha, Sep Makhsous
Manuscript in preparation for *Measurement* (Elsevier) · `IN PREPARATION`

SHIBench is an open benchmark for sensor-fault detection. My contributions were the stress-test rig (Faraday cage and orbital-shaker automation) and the data review before recordings enter the shared dataset.
